How to Play

  1. Look for the Christmas sights listed on your card (page 2).
  2. When you find one, write where you saw it in that square: a street, cross streets, business, or landmark.
  3. The center square is FREE.
  4. Complete any full row, column, or diagonal to get a Bingo.
  5. Write your name and a phone number or email on the card, then turn it in.
