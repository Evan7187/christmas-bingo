How to Play

  1. Look for the Christmas sights listed on your card (page 2).
  2. When you find one, write its location in that square: the street, the nearest cross street or landmark, and a detail. Example: "Main & 3rd, blue house, on the roof."
  3. One great display can count for more than one square. Just write the same location in each.
  4. The center square is FREE.
  5. Complete any full row, column, or diagonal to get a Bingo.
  6. Write your name and a phone number or email on the card, then turn it in.

Rules & Safety

  • Play from sidewalks and public areas. Do not enter private property.
  • Never play while driving. Let a passenger fill in the card.
  • Limit one entry per person. Play on paper or online, not both.
  • Entries are reviewed for completeness. Any honest effort counts!
